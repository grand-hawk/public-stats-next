An **anti-ERA round** is a round built to get past <WikiLink to="explosive-reactive-armour">ERA</WikiLink>. A normal round has to get through the panel's full protection and then the plate behind it. An anti-ERA round gets through the panel as if part of it, or all of it, was not there.

Most anti-ERA rounds are APFSDS with a special tip. A few top-attack missiles and shells have the same ability. Their warheads meet a panel's kinetic protection the way solid shot does, and not its HEAT protection.

Every one of these rounds has an **ERA tip** value on its shell page. APFSDS rounds that have one are also labelled "anti-ERA" in their type.

## How it works

The ERA tip value is how much of a panel's protection against solid shot the round removes. A round with an ERA tip of 1 removes all of it from an old panel. A round with 0.15 removes 15%.

Modern panels fight back. The panel's <Term id="antiTandem" />, the same value that protects it from tandem warheads, reduces the effect. Against Kontakt-5 the reduction is tiny. Against Relikt, Duplet and Monolith it is large. That is what ERA tips above 1 are for.

The exact amount, for any round against any panel:

<Formula>
  protection removed = ERA tip × (1 − tandem resistance<sup>2</sup>)
</Formula>

The result never goes above 100%.

Tandem resistance is the "Against tandem" figure on the ERA page, written as a fraction. For <ShellLink name="DM53" /> against a Relikt front panel that is 0.4 × (1 − 0.8<sup>2</sup>), which comes to 14%.

An example with Kontakt-5:

| Round                       | ERA tip | Protection the panel loses |
| --------------------------- | ------- | -------------------------- |
| Any round without a tip     | None    | 0%                         |
| <ShellLink name="DM53" />   | 0.4     | 39%                        |
| <ShellLink name="M829A3" /> | 1       | 98%                        |

Ordinary HEAT rounds get no anti-ERA effect. For HEAT, see <WikiLink to="tandem-warhead" />.

<Callout type="warning" title="Only the first thing it touches counts">
  The tip only works on the first destructible part the round hits. If a rubber
  skirt or an external fuel tank sits in front of the real ERA, the skirt soaks
  up the effect and the ERA behind it works at full strength.
</Callout>
