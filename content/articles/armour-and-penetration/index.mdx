**Armour and penetration** decide whether a shot gets through. A round goes through a plate when it has more penetration left than the armour in its way is worth. Every [shell page](/shells) lists the penetration of the round, and the pages below cover what changes that figure and what the armour adds up to.

Every plate has a <Term id="ke" /> that counts against all rounds. Some plates and panels also have <Term id="ce" />, which only counts against HEAT warheads. The two are added together, and then the angle of the hit is applied.
