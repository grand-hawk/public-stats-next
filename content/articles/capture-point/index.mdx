A **capture point** is a zone on the map that a team can take and hold. In <WikiLink to="conquest" />, every point your team holds adds to its <Term id="teamScore" />.

## Capturing

Once a second, the game counts the players of each team inside the zone. What matters is your advantage: your head count minus everyone else's combined.

| Situation                         | What happens                                                 |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| You have more players in the zone | The capture moves your way, faster the bigger your advantage |
| Equal numbers                     | The point is contested and nothing moves                     |
| Nobody is there                   | See [Leaving a point](#leaving-a-point)                      |

With an advantage of one player, a neutral point takes 30 seconds to capture. A point the enemy owns takes 60 seconds: 30 to bring it down to neutral and 30 more to make it yours.

More players make it quicker. For example, three players against none capture a neutral point in 10 seconds.

Capturing a point gives up to 250 <WikiLink to="player-score">player score</WikiLink>, split by how much of the capture you did.

## Vehicles count their whole crew

If any seated crew member of a vehicle is inside the zone, everyone in that vehicle counts. A full tank parked on the edge of a point is worth as much as the same crew standing in the middle of it on foot.

## Leaving a point

A point that was only partly captured and then left empty slowly rolls back to neutral, at the same speed a single player would capture it.

A point that is fully yours never decays. It stays yours until the enemy comes and takes it. Finish a capture before you leave: a half-captured point is lost, and a full one holds itself.
