**Conquest** is the main game mode. Several <WikiLink to="capture-point">capture points</WikiLink> are spread across the map, and every point a team holds adds to its <Term id="teamScore" /> each second. The match is decided by comparing team scores at the end.

## How a match plays out

- Each team starts the match already owning the point nearest its spawn.
- The points in between are neutral and have to be captured.
- Holding a point pays out for as long as you keep it. A point taken early is worth more than the same point taken late.
- Kills add to team score as well.

## Winning

When the timer runs out, the game compares your team's score with the other teams' and gives a <WikiLink to="match-result">result</WikiLink>, from Total Victory down to Inconclusive.

See <WikiLink to="team-score">team score</WikiLink> for everything that adds to the score.

A match can also end early. If one team is ahead by 3 to 1 or more when 15 minutes remain, the match stops there.
