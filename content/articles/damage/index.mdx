**Damage** is what a round does once it is through the armour. Getting through is only half the job: what the round does inside decides whether the target keeps fighting.

## Spare penetration

How much damage a round does to the part it hits depends on how much penetration it had to spare.

| The round                                       | Damage it does    |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Has plenty of penetration left after the armour | Full damage       |
| Only just got through                           | 35% of its damage |

Everything in between scales with the penetration left over. If you can choose, shoot the plate your round beats easily, not the one it barely beats.

A hit that fails to penetrate can still do 35% of its damage to the part it struck, as long as the plate is thin compared with the round.

## Spall

Spall is the spray of fragments that comes off the inside of a plate when a round gets through it.

- Heavier shells throw more fragments.
- A hit that only just got through throws more than one that went through easily.
- Each fragment can go through up to 10 mm of armour.

A spall liner is an inner lining that catches fragments. It counts as six times its real thickness against spall.

## HEAT jets

A HEAT jet fades quickly once it is inside, losing 20% of its penetration for every metre. Everything in its line takes full damage, crew included.

## Explosions

An explosion does damage twice: once from fragments and once from the pressure wave.

| Infantry stance | Damage taken |
| --------------- | ------------ |
| Standing        | 100%         |
| Crouching       | 75%          |
| Prone           | 50%          |

A blast can't hurt a vehicle part whose armour is thicker than the blast's own penetration. That is why high explosive does little to a tank's hull.
