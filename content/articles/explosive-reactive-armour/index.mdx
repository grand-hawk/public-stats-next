**Explosive reactive armour** (**ERA**) is an add-on panel that is used up by the hit it stops. In this game the same rules cover every consumable panel: real ERA, rubber flaps, side skirts, <Term id="nera" /> and the composite screens on Western tanks.

A panel gives its full protection against the shot that destroys it. After that, the spot it covered is bare. Two kinds of round are built to get past it: a <WikiLink to="tandem-warhead">tandem warhead</WikiLink> cuts a panel's protection against HEAT, and an <WikiLink to="anti-era-round">anti-ERA round</WikiLink> cuts its protection against solid shot.
