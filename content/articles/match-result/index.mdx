The **match result** is decided when the match ends. The game takes the team with the best <Term id="teamScore" /> and compares it with the average score of the other teams. How far ahead that team is sets the result for everyone.

| The leading team is        | Leading team sees | Other teams see |
| -------------------------- | ----------------- | --------------- |
| At least double the others | Total Victory     | Total Defeat    |
| At least 10% ahead         | Victory           | Defeat          |
| At least 5% ahead          | Minor Victory     | Minor Defeat    |
| Less than 5% ahead         | Inconclusive      | Inconclusive    |

A match can also end early: if one team is ahead by 3 to 1 or more when 15 minutes remain, it stops there.

## Examples

With two teams:

| Your team | Enemy team | Your result   |
| --------- | ---------- | ------------- |
| 24,000    | 11,000     | Total Victory |
| 18,000    | 15,000     | Victory       |
| 15,900    | 15,000     | Minor Victory |
| 15,300    | 15,000     | Inconclusive  |
| 12,000    | 15,000     | Defeat        |

With three teams, the leader is compared with the average of the other two. For example, a team on 20,000 against teams on 12,000 and 6,000 is compared with 9,000. That is more than double, which makes it a Total Victory.

## XP bonus for winning

Players on the winning team get bonus XP on top of what they earned in the match. The bonus depends on the result, not on the exact scores.

| Result        | Bonus XP |
| ------------- | -------- |
| Total Victory | +100%    |
| Victory       | +20%     |
| Minor Victory | +10%     |
| Inconclusive  | None     |

The bonus does not raise the Money you earn.
