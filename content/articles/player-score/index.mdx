**Player score** is your own score for the current match. It decides which vehicle <WikiLink to="tier">tiers</WikiLink> you can spawn, and it starts again from zero every match.

It is separate from <WikiLink to="team-score" />, which is shared by the whole team and decides who wins.

## What earns it

| Action                  | Score                                                                        |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Kill a player           | 75, and each of your crewmates gets 75 too                                   |
| Destroy a vehicle       | 150 to 450 depending on its tier, paid to your whole crew                    |
| Assist                  | 50                                                                           |
| Knock out a crew member | 50                                                                           |
| Capture a point         | Up to 250, split by how much of the capture you did                          |
| Revive a teammate       | 50                                                                           |
| Spot an enemy           | 25, once per target                                                          |
| Quick reload            | 25                                                                           |
| Repair                  | 5, plus 1 for each step of the repair                                        |
| Resupply                | Up to 15                                                                     |
| Transport passengers    | As the driver, 5 per passenger for every 30 m travelled                      |
| Team score milestone    | 750 for everyone on the team, each time its team score passes another 10,000 |

### Destroying vehicles

| Tier of the vehicle | Score |
| ------------------- | ----- |
| 1                   | 150   |
| 2                   | 200   |
| 3                   | 250   |
| 4                   | 300   |
| 5                   | 350   |
| 6                   | 400   |
| 7                   | 450   |

## Score over time

Every player gets 50 score for each minute of the match, whatever they are doing.

If you join a match that is already running, you are given the 50 per minute you missed. This is skipped when less than two and a half minutes are left. If you leave and rejoin the same match, you get back the score you had.

## XP

Score from the actions in the table is also added to your XP. Score over time is not: it unlocks tiers and gives no XP.
