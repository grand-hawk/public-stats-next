A **ricochet** is a round glancing off a plate without going in. Whether it can happen depends on the round.

| Round         | When it bounces                                                                                                          |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| AP and APHE   | By chance, in a zone that begins just under the ricochet angle on the shell page. The zone is narrower for faster rounds |
| APFSDS        | Only off plates thicker than its penetrator. Thin plates can't deflect it                                                |
| HEAT          | Off any plate of 5 mm or more. It carries on with 90% of its penetration                                                 |
| HE and others | Depends on weight and speed. Heavy shells dig into thin plates and don't bounce                                          |

For AP and APHE, the distance flown before the bounce still counts against the round's <WikiLink to="penetration#distance">penetration</WikiLink>.
