A **ricochet** is a round glancing off a plate without going in. Each shell page lists a ricochet angle, and what that angle means depends on the round.

| Round         | When it bounces                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| AP            | Always at its ricochet angle or steeper. Below that there is a zone where it bounces by chance. The zone is wide for slow shells and gone above about 1,900 m/s |
| APHE          | Only at its ricochet angle or steeper                                                                                                                           |
| APFSDS        | At its ricochet angle or steeper, off a plate at least as thick as its penetrator. A thinner plate can still deflect it at an even steeper angle                |
| HEAT          | At its ricochet angle or steeper, off any plate worth 5 mm or more                                                                                              |
| HE and others | At their ricochet angle or steeper, if the plate is thick enough for the weight of the shell                                                                    |

For example, an AP shell at 795 m/s with a ricochet angle of 65° can start to bounce from 44°.

AP and APHE also have to pass the weight test. A heavy shell digs into a thin plate and does not bounce: a 25 kg shell needs roughly 75 to 150 mm of plate to be deflected.

## After the bounce

| Round       | Penetration kept                      |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------- |
| AP and APHE | Very little, often close to nothing   |
| APFSDS      | 25% to 90%, less for a steeper bounce |
| HEAT        | 90%                                   |

The distance flown before the bounce still counts against the <WikiLink to="penetration#distance">penetration</WikiLink> of solid shot.
