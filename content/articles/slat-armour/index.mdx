**Slat armour** is a cage fitted around a vehicle. It only affects HEAT warheads and rockets.

<Figure
  src={slat}
  alt="A T-90M seen from behind, with slat armour around the rear of the turret and the rear of the hull"
  caption="Slat armour around the rear of a T-90M's turret and hull."
/>

| Hit by                | What slat armour does                                                   |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| HEAT shell or missile | Takes about 16% off its penetration                                     |
| Top-attack EFP        | Takes 40% off its penetration                                           |
| Infantry rocket       | 40% chance to stop it outright. Otherwise takes 40% off its penetration |
| Solid shot            | Nothing                                                                 |

In the Modern loadout, crew can also place slat armour on their own vehicle.
