**Slat armour** is a cage fitted around a vehicle. It only affects HEAT warheads and rockets.

<Figure
  src={slat}
  alt="A T-90M seen from behind, with slat armour around the rear of the turret and the rear of the hull"
  caption="Slat armour around the rear of a T-90M's turret and hull."
/>

Every cage has a slat value. It is 40% on most vehicles, 20% on a few and 60% on one.

| Hit by                                                    | What slat armour does                                         |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Infantry rocket                                           | A chance equal to the slat value to stop it outright          |
| HEAT jet that reaches the cage after the warhead went off | Takes 16% off its penetration at the usual slat value         |
| EFP that reaches the cage after the warhead went off      | Takes 40% off its penetration at the usual slat value         |
| HEAT shell, missile or EFP that strikes the cage itself   | Almost nothing. It only loses the few millimetres of the cage |
| Solid shot                                                | Nothing                                                       |

In the Modern loadout, crew can also place slat armour on their own vehicle.
