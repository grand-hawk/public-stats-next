A **tandem warhead** is a HEAT warhead with a small charge in front. The small charge sets off <WikiLink to="explosive-reactive-armour">ERA</WikiLink> early, and the main charge then meets less of the panel.

## What it changes

A tandem warhead only does one thing differently from ordinary HEAT: it cuts the HEAT protection of add-on panels. How much is left depends on the panel's tandem resistance. Old panels have none, and modern ones keep most of their protection.

- It works on every panel in its path, not only the first one.
- It does not touch a panel's protection against solid shot, which still counts in full.
- It does nothing extra against armour that is built into the hull or turret. Composite armour keeps all of its HEAT protection.

In every other way it is a normal HEAT round. <WikiLink to="slat-armour">Slat armour</WikiLink> weakens it, it can <WikiLink to="ricochet">ricochet</WikiLink>, and its jet fades inside the vehicle in the same way (see <WikiLink to="damage#heat-jets">HEAT jets</WikiLink>).
