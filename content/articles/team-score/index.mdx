**Team score** is the number that decides who wins a match. It is shared by the whole team, and at the end of the match the team scores are compared to give the <WikiLink to="match-result">match result</WikiLink>.

It is separate from your <WikiLink to="player-score">player score</WikiLink>, which decides which vehicle <Term id="tier">tiers</Term> you can spawn.

## Where it comes from

| Source                                                                     | Score             |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Each <WikiLink to="capture-point">capture point</WikiLink> your team holds | 10 every second   |
| Every kill reward earned by anyone on your team                            | 25% of the reward |

To compare the two: one point held for a minute is worth 600. Destroying a tier 4 vehicle pays its killers 300, which adds 75 to the team.

Each time the score passes another 10,000, everyone on the team gets 750 player score.
