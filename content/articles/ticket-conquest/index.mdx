**Ticket Conquest** is a game mode for two teams in which each side starts the match with 1,000 tickets. Holding fewer <WikiLink to="capture-point">capture points</WikiLink> than the enemy drains your pool, and every vehicle your team loses costs tickets as well. A team that reaches zero loses there and then.

## What tickets cost you

| What                                | Tickets                                                         |
| ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Holding fewer points than the enemy | A quarter of a ticket per second for every point you are behind |
| Losing a vehicle                    | 10, however it was destroyed                                    |
| Losing a jeep, truck or half-track  | 3                                                               |

The cheaper rate is for the transports and supply trucks only. An armoured personnel carrier counts as a full vehicle.

Only the gap counts. While both teams hold the same number of points, neither side loses anything, and the team ahead on points never bleeds.

A vehicle that despawns because it was left empty does not count. One that despawns after its crew was wiped out, or while it burns with nobody inside, still costs its tickets.

| Points behind | Tickets per minute | Empties the pool in |
| ------------- | ------------------ | ------------------- |
| 1             | 15                 | Longer than a match |
| 2             | 30                 | 33 minutes          |
| 3             | 45                 | 22 minutes          |

Vehicle losses come on top of that. A team that trades badly can empty its pool long before the bleed would.

Unlike <WikiLink to="conquest" />, holding a point builds no <WikiLink to="team-score">team score</WikiLink> here. Points only matter through the bleed.

## Score

Each time the enemy's losses pass another 200 tickets, your team gets up to 750 <WikiLink to="player-score">player score</WikiLink> each. You get the full amount if you were on the team for the whole stretch since the last payout, or for the last 5 minutes of it. Joining partway through gets you a share of it.

## Helicopters

Helicopters are locked while your team still holds more than 800 tickets. They open up once your side is 200 tickets down.

## Winning

Running the enemy out of tickets ends the match on the spot, and counts as a Total Victory.

If the 45 minute timer ends first, tickets take the place of team score when the <WikiLink to="match-result">match result</WikiLink> is worked out: whoever has more tickets left wins, and the margin decides how big the win is. A team that is 3 to 1 ahead on tickets with 15 minutes left ends the match early.
