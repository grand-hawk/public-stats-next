Every vehicle has a **tier** from 1 to 7. A tier is the <WikiLink to="player-score">player score</WikiLink> you need before you can spawn it. It is a threshold and not a price: spawning never spends your score.

| Tier | Score needed |
| ---- | ------------ |
| 1    | 0            |
| 2    | 1,250        |
| 3    | 2,500        |
| 4    | 3,750        |
| 5    | 5,000        |
| 6    | 7,500        |
| 7    | 10,000       |

Score starts again from zero every match. Everyone begins in tier 1 vehicles.

Addons you pick for a vehicle add their cost to the score you need. Infantry kits use the same table, and the highest-tier item in the kit sets the score.

After you spawn a vehicle, you have to wait its tier times 24 seconds before spawning that vehicle again. For example, a tier 5 vehicle has a 2 minute cooldown.
