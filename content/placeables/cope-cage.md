# Cope Cage

The **Cope Cage** is a large slatted roof on tall legs that a crewman can fix over a vehicle's turret as [[/slat-armour slat armour]]. It covers the turret roof, where most vehicles are thinnest, and gives an infantry rocket coming in from above a fair chance of being stopped by the slats. It does next to nothing against shells and missiles that hit the cage directly.
