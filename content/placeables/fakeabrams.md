# FakeAbrams

The **Fake M1 Abrams** is a flat cutout of the front of an [[/vehicles/m1-abrams M1 Abrams]] on a wooden base that an engineer can stand up on the ground as a decoy. From a distance it looks like a real tank and can draw enemy fire, but it takes almost nothing to destroy.
