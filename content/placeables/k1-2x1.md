# K1 2x1

The **K1 2x1** is a pair of Kontakt-1 [[/explosive-reactive-armour explosive reactive armour]] blocks that a crewman can fix to a vehicle. The blocks explode outward when a HEAT round or missile hits them and break up most of the jet before it reaches the hull. They do nothing against a kinetic round, and a [[/tandem-warhead tandem warhead]] goes straight through.
