# Metal Wedge

The **Metal Wedge** is a wedge-shaped steel frame with sloped plates that a crewman can fix to a vehicle. The plates are thin, but set at an angle they have more [[/line-of-sight-thickness line-of-sight thickness]] than they would lying flat.

Unlike most placeables, other placeables can be fixed on top of it, so it works as a sloped mount for reactive armour or other add-ons. That only goes one layer deep, so a wedge that sits on another placeable takes nothing more.
