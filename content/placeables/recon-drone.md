# Recon Drone

The **Recon Drone** is an unarmed quadcopter for scouting. A player places it on the ground, then flies it from a controller, with a stabilised camera that can zoom in to scout enemies from far away.

## Flying

The drone's battery drains faster the harder it flies, and it falls out of the sky when it runs flat. Its video and control signal weaken with distance from the pilot and through walls and terrain, and a [[/placeables/volnorez Volnorez]] or other jammer nearby can cut the link entirely. Enemies can shoot the drone down.

After deploying one, a player has to wait 45 seconds before deploying another.
