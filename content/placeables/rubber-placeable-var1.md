# Rubber Placeable var1

The first variant of the **Rubber Placeable** is a flat rubber skirt panel on a mounting bar that a crewman can fix to a vehicle. It does little against anything, though it takes a bit more out of HEAT than out of kinetic rounds. The [[/placeables/rubber-placeable-var2 second variant]] is the same panel with a curved edge.
