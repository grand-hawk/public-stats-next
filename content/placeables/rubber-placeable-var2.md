# Rubber Placeable var2

The second variant of the **Rubber Placeable** is a rubber skirt panel on a mounting bar with its far edge curved down. A crewman can fix it to a vehicle. It does little against anything, though it takes a bit more out of HEAT than out of kinetic rounds. The [[/placeables/rubber-placeable-var1 first variant]] is the same panel laid flat.
