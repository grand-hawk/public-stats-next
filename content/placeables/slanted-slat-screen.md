# Slanted Slat Screen

The **Slanted Slat Screen** is a panel of steel slats on legs of different heights, so it sits at a slope, that a crewman can fix to a vehicle as [[/slat-armour slat armour]]. It stands off from the hull, so an infantry rocket has a fair chance of being stopped by the slats before it reaches the armour. It does next to nothing against shells and missiles that hit the screen directly.
