# TrenchBunker

The **Trench Bunker** is a length of trench roofed over with a thick layer of earth, giving the players inside cover from above. An engineer can dig it into the ground, and placed near the open end of another trench piece, it snaps on to continue the line.

A trench is permanent. Once dug, it cannot be destroyed or removed, and stays for the rest of the match.
