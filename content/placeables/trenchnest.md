# TrenchNest

The **Trench Nest** is a dead end of trench with a raised wooden platform at the closed end, a firing position for players to shoot out over the top. An engineer can dig it into the ground, and placed near the open end of another trench piece, it snaps on to close off the line.

A trench is permanent. Once dug, it cannot be destroyed or removed, and stays for the rest of the match.
