# TrenchStand

The **Trench Stand** is a straight length of trench with a raised wooden fire step along one wall, so players can step up and shoot over the top. An engineer can dig it into the ground, and placed near the open end of another trench piece, it snaps on to continue the line.

A trench is permanent. Once dug, it cannot be destroyed or removed, and stays for the rest of the match.
