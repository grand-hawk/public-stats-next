# TrenchStraight

The **Trench Straight** is a straight length of trench, with timber-lined walls and earth banked up on both sides, that an engineer can dig into the ground. Placed near the open end of another trench piece, it snaps on to continue the line, so straights, turns and T-pieces can be joined into a full trench network.

A trench is permanent. Once dug, it cannot be destroyed or removed, and stays for the rest of the match.
