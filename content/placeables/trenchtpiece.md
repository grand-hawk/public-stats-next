# TrenchTPiece

The **Trench T-Piece** is a junction of trench with three open ends, with timber-lined walls and earth banked up around it, that an engineer can dig into the ground. Placed near the open end of another trench piece, it snaps on and splits the line in two, so a trench can branch off in a new direction.

A trench is permanent. Once dug, it cannot be destroyed or removed, and stays for the rest of the match.
