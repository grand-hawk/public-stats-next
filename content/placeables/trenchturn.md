# TrenchTurn

The **Trench Turn** is a right-angled corner of trench, with timber-lined walls and earth banked up around it, that an engineer can dig into the ground. Placed near the open end of another trench piece, it snaps on and turns the line by 90 degrees.

A trench is permanent. Once dug, it cannot be destroyed or removed, and stays for the rest of the match.
