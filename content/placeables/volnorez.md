# Volnorez

The **Volnorez** is a drone jammer that a crewman can fix to a vehicle. It floods the area around it with noise that drowns out the signal between an FPV drone and its pilot.

## Jamming

The closer a drone flies to the Volnorez, the worse its signal gets, and the effect drops off quickly with distance. Walls, hulls and terrain between the jammer and the drone block part of it, so a drone coming in low behind cover is jammed less than one in the open.

As the signal gets worse, the pilot sees it in stages.

- The video picks up static, which thickens the closer the drone gets.
- The controls start to twitch, with some of the pilot's inputs lost on the way to the drone.
- The video cuts out and the drone loses its link. It holds its last input for half a second, then cuts its motors and falls.

Jamming near the drone hits both its video and its controls. A jammer near the pilot only hurts the video, since the pilot's commands still reach the drone.

The jammer does not tell friend from foe, and it jams FPV drones from its own team just as much as the enemy's.
