# Felid Empire

The **Felid Empire** represents a modern island nation of the far east in the [[/loadouts/modern Modern]] loadout, with its vehicles playable in-game under the [[/teams/eagle-federation Eagle Federation]]. In [[/loadouts/ww2 World War 2]], the Felid Empire stands for the wartime island empire and the territories it conquered during the war.
